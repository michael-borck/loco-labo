## Why this lab exists

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Mapping the floor" icon="down-caret">
    Most AI research documents the ceiling. We document the floor: what small local models can do on modest hardware, because that is where most people live.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local and private by design" icon="approve-check">
    Local inference keeps student work, research data, and private conversations on a machine you control. No token tax, no data leaving the room.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversation, not delegation" icon="comment">
    A small model that keeps you thinking beats a big one that thinks for you. Read [the Loco thesis](/the-loco-thesis/) for the principles behind every project.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open to explorers" icon="rocket">
    Everything is public: code, data, and failures. Try a [quest](/quests/), or contribute a measured result to the [GPU registry](/gpu-registry/) and LocoBanco.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
