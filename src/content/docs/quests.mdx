Quests are trails through the valley. Each one strings together pages across the LocoLabo sites toward something you can actually do: run a model, wake an old GPU, or add a measured result.

Clicking a step's link counts it as visited. A few steps happen on your own machine, so you tick those yourself. Progress and badges are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.

<QuestBoard />

Prefer plain documentation? Every project is listed on [All Projects](/projects/), and every page links back here from its footer.
