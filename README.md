# LocoLabo

> Frontier AI on a budget. Crazy, right?

LocoLabo is an independent, self-funded applied AI research lab in Perth, Australia. We study what local AI can do on modest hardware, and build tools that put that capability in the hands of educators, students, and organisations that can't or won't send their data to the cloud.

The thesis is **Conversation, not Delegation**: for the most educationally valuable uses of AI, a small model that keeps the human thinking is the right tool, not a compromise. Local inference also keeps student and research data on-premises by design.
